# Game design

Start with Rp 20,000, eight residents, two houses, a rice field, village hall and a short road. Expand from 16×16 owned tiles to the full 40×40 map. Earn income through trade and employed residents. Keep rice available, build connected houses and services, then expand livestock and processing.

A day is 120 simulation seconds. Crops take 24–72 seconds, construction at most 45 seconds. Player actions grant XP. Unlocks are content driven. Eras: levels 1–10 Dusun Perintis; 11–30 Desa Berkembang; 31–60 Desa Mandiri; 61–100 Pusat Perdagangan; 101–160 Kota Industri; 161–220 Kota Modern; 221+ Metropolitan.

Twenty-one introductory/growth quests plus seven multi-objective era milestone quests accompany renewable market contracts. Seven technologies provide measurable multipliers. At level 250 prestige starts a fresh village with additional starting capital. Level data currently ends at 300. Playtime/retention is unmeasured; late-game variety needs playtesting and further authored content.

Optional ads are disabled in this candidate. There is no premium currency, login or internet requirement. Bankruptcy has a community-work recovery contract (Rp 500 and five seeds, costs 30 simulation seconds, available below Rp 800).
