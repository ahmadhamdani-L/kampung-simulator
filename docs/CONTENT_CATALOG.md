# Content catalog

Authoritative definitions are in `data/` and validated by `tools/validate_content.py`.

* 52 buildings across housing, farming, livestock, commerce, industry, services and infrastructure, each with level, cost, material, footprint, construction, capacity/jobs, maintenance and upgrades.
* Seven crop fields: rice, corn, chili, cassava, banana, coconut, coffee.
* Six livestock operations: chicken, duck, goat, cow, catfish, tilapia.
* Seven industry recipes: rice milling, tofu, tempe, flour, prepared food and lumber processing.
* Seven technologies with prerequisites, integer purchase requirements and yield/growth/production/trade/happiness effects.
* 28 ordered quests, repeatable contracts, eight achievements, 300 level thresholds and seven development eras.
* Seven weighted timed events: harvest festival, rain, drought, pests, flood, tourism, labor shortage. Prerequisites and cooldowns constrain selection.

Some building descriptions and English names are generated from identifiers. Production art/localization and content-specific differentiation remain review tasks. Tests validate content structure, references, dependencies and ordering; they do not establish narrative quality.
